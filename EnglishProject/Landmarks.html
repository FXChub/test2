Quick Facts

  • California is home to many famous landmarks. If its the Golden Gate Bridge and Alcatraz in San Francisco, the Hollywood sign and Universal Studios in LA or Comic Con and the San Diego Zoo in said city, there is always something to see and visit in California.
  • California has the highest number of national parks in the US. It has 9 big parks featuring large and iconic ones such as Yosemite, Death Valley and Joshua Tree. It also contains multiple other parks that may not be as popular, but just as pretty with the Lassen Volcanic and Redwood parks. Not every park is enormous though as Alcatraz Island outside San Francisco is also a national park.
  • Californias 3 biggest cities are Los Angeles, San Jose and San Diego. 2 of the previously mentioned cities are the most popular when it comes to tourist. not surprisingly are those Los Angeles and San Diego. San Francisco also makes its debut in the list when its the second most popular. Other cities such as Sacramento, Santa Monica, Palm Springs and Anaheim are also popular for various reasons, but that is saved for the attractions page.
